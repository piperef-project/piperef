Basics

#76Which code covers process piping in refineries and chemical plants?
  1. ASME B31.1
  2. ASME B31.8
  3. ASME B31.3
  4. ASME B31.4

Answer: ASME B31.3. B31.3 is process piping. B31.1 is power piping, B31.4 liquid pipelines and B31.8 gas transmission.

#79Why are molten sulphur lines steam-jacketed?
  1. To lower pressure drop
  2. To stop corrosion
  3. For fire protection
  4. Sulphur solidifies if it cools below about 115 °C

Answer: Sulphur solidifies if it cools below about 115 °C. Sulphur melts at about 115 °C, so the jacket keeps it hot enough to flow without freezing in the line.

#80What does a Claus sulphur recovery unit recover?
  1. Hydrogen from water
  2. Diesel from crude
  3. Elemental sulphur from H₂S
  4. Steam from flue gas

Answer: Elemental sulphur from H₂S. The Claus process turns hydrogen sulphide from the refinery’s gas streams into elemental sulphur.

#82Which refinery unit removes sulphur compounds from products?
  1. Fluid catalytic cracker
  2. Hydrotreater
  3. Crude distillation unit
  4. Coker

Answer: Hydrotreater. Hydrotreaters react the sulphur compounds with hydrogen to make H₂S, which then goes to the sulphur recovery unit.

Answer: Lets condensate out while keeping steam in. Traps drain condensate from steam lines and equipment without letting live steam escape.

#1Which valve should you never use to throttle flow?
  1. Gate valve
  2. Globe valve
  3. Needle valve
  4. Butterfly valve

Answer: Gate valve. A gate valve is made to be fully open or fully shut. Part-open, flow squeezes under the wedge and wears the seats until it leaks. Throttle with a globe valve.
